LatoChan gif folders
====================
Put gif LINKS (not files) in each mood folder's links.txt, one per line.
Tenor / Klipy / Giphy share links all work: Discord turns the link into the gif.
Then upload them with the LatoDash GIFs tab, or copy the folder to /var/lib/latochan/gifs/ on the server (no restart needed).

waking  = sometimes posted when someone wakes her up (about 30% of wake-ups)
all moods = she can pick one with [[GIF: mood]] when it fits.
Empty folders are fine: she just skips the gif.
